About AutoTrace

Transparent Vehicle History at Your Fingertips

AutoTrace is a modern, responsive frontend application designed to showcase comprehensive vehicle inspection reports, ownership history, and health diagnostics in a clean, professional dashboard.

Comprehensive History

Analyze complete ownership trails, multi-state registrations, insurance standings, and official document validity dates.

AI Health Scoring

Inspect multi-point health ratings covering engine performance, transmission smoothness, body panels, and mileage consistency checks.

Service & Maintenance Logs

Review authorized service center records, odometer mileages at each interval, part replacements, and repair costs.

Accident & Damage Records

Examine severity classifications, insurance claim histories, structural repair records, and legal/theft status checks.

Frontend Architecture & Technical Highlights

  • Pure Vanilla Stack: Built using HTML5, CSS3, and ES6+ JavaScript without external framework bloat.
  • Persistent State: Utilizes browser localStorage for saving favorite vehicles and recently viewed items across sessions.
  • Print-Friendly Design: Generates clean, formatted PDF or physical printouts directly from browser layout engines.
  • Responsive UI: Optimized for mobile phones, tablets, and widescreen desktop displays with dark theme support.
